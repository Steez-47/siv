STZ 1.5.01 Async save button

Use: Any network action. Shows busy spinner, then a success state, then resets. Replace the timer with your real promise.

Anim: label blurs out, spinner scales in, then check + 'Saved' (dur-2 spring)
JS: data-async="ms" → stz:confirm when done. Real use: set el.dataset.state = "busy" | "done" | "idle" yourself.
